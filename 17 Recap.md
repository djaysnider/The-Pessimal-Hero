I guess we’ll call that running ... but I was still able to make out exactly where you were without going to the GPS.  Okay, so we only tested it over a block or two before you got winded and almost passed out, but still, good footage, man.  Can’t wait to show it to you.”

You really should have consulted with us before you picked your stupid superhero name.  We completely could have helped with an entire marketing and PR angle.”
